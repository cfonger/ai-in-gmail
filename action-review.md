## Context

Use the installed Gmail MCP server to access Gmail. Follow the classification rules in .claude/CLAUDE.md.

## Your task

Review my action items and help me work through them, like a chief of staff running through my task list.

### Step 1: Gather action items

Search for emails with these labels (always include `in:anywhere` so snoozed/archived items aren't silently excluded):

- "01 Action" (`label:01-action in:anywhere`)
- "00 Action - Cust" (`label:00-action---cust in:anywhere`) — note **three dashes** because the label name has space-dash-space

For each email, read the full thread to understand context.

### Step 2: Present prioritized list

Present all action items ranked by importance (NOT just age). Consider:

- **Urgency**: Deadlines, time-sensitive requests, legal/compliance
- **Impact**: Customer revenue at risk, blocking others, strategic importance
- **Effort**: Quick wins vs. deep work (flag the 2-minute tasks)

For each item, show:

- Subject and sender
- What action is needed (be specific)
- Why you ranked it where you did
- If it's a 2-minute task, flag it — GTD says do those immediately

### Step 3: Work through them together

For each action item, ask me what I'd like to do:

- **Reply**: Draft a reply together (follow drafting rules in .claude/CLAUDE.md)
- **Delegate**: Draft a forwarding email to someone else
- **Defer**: Suggest a snooze time, add context for future-me
- **Done**: Remove the action label, archive it
- **Skip**: Move to the next one

Work through them one at a time. After each action, move to the next item.

### Step 4: Summary

After we've gone through everything, give a wrap-up:

- What was completed
- What was deferred (and when it'll come back)
- What's still pending
- Any patterns you noticed (e.g., "3 customers waiting on the same feature")

## Important rules

- Never delete emails. Only add/remove labels.
- When drafting replies, never use emdashes. Use commas, periods, or semicolons instead.
- Be opinionated about priority — I want your judgment, not just a list.
